Product Value Proposition: Trusted C2C Resale MarketplaceOne-Line Pitch (Elevator Pitch)"Bangladesh’s first verified peer-to-peer marketplace—where every buyer and seller is NID-authenticated to eliminate scams, stolen goods, and ghost deals."1. The Core ProblemCurrent classified sites (like Bikroy or Facebook Marketplace) rely on anonymous profiles. Anyone can post an ad using a temporary SIM card or a fake profile. This leads to:Advance payment and delivery scams.Fear of buying stolen electronics or fake goods.Harassment and unsafe in-person meetups.2. The Solution: Dual-Sided NID VerificationWe keep the familiar classifieds workflow (browse, chat, negotiate, deal) but introduce a mandatory identity layer:Seller Verification: Every seller must verify their identity via National ID (NID) and a live selfie before posting an ad.Buyer Verification: Serious buyers verify via NID before initiating chat or making offers.Verified Badges: Both parties trade with real identity transparency, creating natural accountability.3.
